The General Epistle of Barnabas — Chapter 1

1 Preface to the Epistle. ALL happiness to you my sons and daughters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, who loved us, in peace.
2 Having perceived abundance of knowledge of the great and   a excellent   b laws of God to be in you, I exceedingly rejoice in your blessed and admirable   c souls, because ye have so worthily received the grace which was   d grafted in you.
3 For which cause I am full of joy, hoping the rather to he   a saved; inasmuch as I truly see a spirit infused into you, from the   b pure fountain of God:
4 Having this persuasion, and being fully convinced thereof, because that since I have begun to speak unto you, I have had a more than ordinary good success in the way of   a the law of the Lord which is in Christ.
5 For which cause   a brethren, I also think verily that I love you above my own soul: because that therein dwelleth the greatness of faith and charity, as also the hope of that life which is to come.
6 Wherefore considering this, that if I shall take care to communicate to you a part of what I have received, it shall turn to my reward,   a that I have served such good souls; I gave diligence to write in a few words unto you; that together with your faith,   b knowledge also may be perfect.
7 There are therefore three   a things ordained by the Lord; the hope of life;   b the beginning and the completion of it.
8 For the Lord hath both declared unto us, by the prophets those things that   a are past; and   b opened to us the beginnings of those that are to come.
9 Wherefore, it will behoove us,   a as he has spoken, to come   b more holily, and nearer to his altar.
10 I therefore, not as a teacher, but as one   a of you, will endeavour to lay before you a few things by which you may, on   b many accounts, become the more joyful.
