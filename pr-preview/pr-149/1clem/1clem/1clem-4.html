The First Epistle of Clement to the Corinthians — Chapter 4

1 He exhorts them to live by the rules, and repent of their divisions, and they shall be forgiven.

1 THESE things, beloved, we   a write unto you, not only   b for your instruction, but also for our own remembrance.
2 For we are all in the same   a lists, and the same combat is   b prepared for us all.
3 Wherefore let us lay aside all vain and empty cares; and let us come up to the glorious and venerable rule of our holy calling.
4 a Let us consider what is good, and acceptable and well-pleasing in the sight of him that made us.
5 Let us look steadfastly to the blood of Christ, and see how precious his blood is in the sight of God: which being shed for our salvation,  a has obtained the grace of repentance for all the world.
6 Let us   a search into all the ages that have gone before us; and let us learn that our Lord has   b in every one of them still given place for repentance to all such as would   c turn to him.
7 a Noah preached repentance; and as many as hearkened to him were saved.   b Jonah denounced destruction against the Ninevites:
8 Howbeit they repenting of their sins, appeased God by their prayers: and   a were saved, though they were strangers to the covenant of God.
9 Hence we find how all the ministers of the grace of God have spoken by the Holy Spirit of repentance. And even the Lord of all has himself   a declared with an oath concerning it;
10 a As I live, saith the Lord, I desire not the death of a sinner,   b but that he should repent. Adding farther this good sentence, saying:   c Turn from your iniquity, O house of Israel.
11 a Say unto the children of my people, Though your sins should reach from earth to heaven; and though they shall be redder than scarlet, and blacker than sackcloth; yet if ye shall turn to me with all your heart, and shall call me father, I will hearken to you, as to a holy people.
12 And in another place he saith on this wise:   a Wash ye, make you clean; put away   b the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil, learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.
13 Come now and let us reason together, saith the Lord: though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; though they he red as crimson,   a they shall be as wool.
14 If ye he willing and obedient, ye shall eat the good of the land; but if ye refuse and rebel, ye shall be devoured with the sword; for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.
15 These things has God established by his Almighty will, desiring that all his beloved should come to repentance.
