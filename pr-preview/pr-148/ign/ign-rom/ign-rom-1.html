The Epistle of Ignatius to the Romans — Chapter 1

He testifies his desire to see, and his hopes of suffering for Christ, 5 which he earnestly entreats them not to prevent, 10 but to pray for him, that God would strengthen him to the combat.

1 IGNATIUS,   a who is also called Theophorus, to the church which has obtained mercy   b from the majesty of the Most High Father, and his only   c begotten Son Jesus Christ; beloved, and illuminated   d through the will of who willeth all things which are according to the love of Jesus Christ our   e God which also presides in the   f place of the region of the Romans; and which   g I salute in the name of Jesus Christ (  h as being) united both in flesh and spirit to all his commands, and   i filled with the grace of God;   j (all joy) in Jesus Christ our God.
2 a Forasmuch as I have at last   b obtained through my prayers to God, to see your   c faces,   d which I much desired to do; being bound in Jesus Christ, I hope ere long to salute you, if it shall be the will   e of God to grant me to attain unto the end I long for.
3 For the beginning is well disposed, if I shall but have grace, without hindrance, to receive   a what is appointed for me.
4 But I fear your love, lest it do me an injury. For it is easy for you to do what you please; but it   a will be hard for me to attain unto God, if you spare me.
5 But I   a would not that ye should please men, but God   b whom also ye do please. For neither shall I hereafter have such an opportunity   c of going unto God; nor will you if ye shall now be silent, ever be entitled to a better work. For if you shall be silent   d in my behalf, I shall be made partaker of God.
6 But if you shall love my   a body, I shall have my course again to run. Wherefore ye cannot do me a greater kindness, than to suffer me to be sacrificed unto God, now that the altar is already prepared:
7 That   a when ye shall be gathered together in love, ye may   b give thanks to the Father through Christ Jesus; that he has vouchsafed   c to bring a bishop of Syria unto you, being called from the east unto the west.
8 For it is good for me to set from the world, unto God; that I may rise again unto him.
9 Ye have never envied any one; ye have taught other. I would therefore that ye   a should now do those things yourselves, which in your instructions you have   b prescribed to others.
10 Only pray for me, that God would give me both inward and outward strength, that I may not only say, but will; nor be only called a Christian, but be found one.
11 For if I shall be found a Christian, I may then deservedly be called one; and be thought faithful, when I shall no longer appear to the world.
12 Nothing is   a good, that is seen.
13 For even our God, Jesus Christ, now that he is in the Father, does so much the more appear.
14 A Christian is not a work of   a opinion; but of greatness of mind, (  b especially when he is hated by the world.)
