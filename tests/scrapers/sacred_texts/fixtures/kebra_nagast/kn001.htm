THE GLORY OF KINGS

IN PRAISING GOD THE FATHER, THE SUSTAINER OF THE UNIVERSE, AND HIS SON JESUS CHRIST, WE BELIEVE IN AND ADORE THE TRINITY.

1. Concerning the Glory of Kings

   The interpretation and explanation of the Three Hundred and Eighteen Orthodox [Fathers] concerning splendour, and how God gave them to the children of ADAM. And then the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit said, "Let Us make man in Our similitude and likeness,"1 and with ready agreement They were all of this opinion. p. 2 And DAVID said, "Remember Thine agreement which Thou didst make of old."1

   And He made ADAM in His own image—His own plant—for His praises.2
