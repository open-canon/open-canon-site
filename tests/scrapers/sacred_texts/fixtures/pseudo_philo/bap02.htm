1. GENERAL.

1. THE book now presented to English readers has never been translated before.

Its importance lies in this, that it is a genuine Jewish book of the first century, and written in the years which followed the destruction of

Jerusalem in A.D. 70. It is thus contemporary with some of the New Testament writings.
