CHAPTER XLII.

XLII. Now there was a man of the tribe of
Jud. 13:9
Dan, whose name was Manue. And she said: Let the law make plain our trial. 1 2. And as they contended day by day they prayed saying: Do thou, O Lord God of all flesh, reveal unto me whether unto my husband or unto me it is not given to beget children.

3. But Manue and his wife fell upon their faces and said: We shall surely die. Now the angel that came was called Phadahel.

XLIII. And it came to pass in the time of
Jud. 13:24
those days, that Eluma conceived and bare a son and called his name Samson.

2. And after that Samson entered into (or was enraged against) Azotus. And they shut him in

p. 198

and compassed the city about. 3. And he went and set his left hand under the bar of the gate and killed therewith 25,000 men.

4. Now concerning the lion which he slew, are not these things written in the book of the Judges?
