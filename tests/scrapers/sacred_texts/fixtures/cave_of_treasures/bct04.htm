THE CAVE OF TREASURES

TRANSLATION

(Brit. Mus. MS. Add. 25875.)

[THE TITLE OF THE WORK: THE SCRIBE'S PRAYER.]

[Fol. 3b, col. 1.] By the might of our Lord Jesus Christ we begin to write the "Book of the Succession of the Generations," that is to say, ME`ÂRATH GAZZÊ, which was composed by SAINT MÂR APHRÊM (i.e. Ephraim). Amen.

[THE FIRST THOUSAND YEARS: ADAM TO YARÊD (Jared).]

[The Creation. First Day.]

In the beginning, on the First Day, God created the heavens, p. 44 and the earth, and the waters.

[NOTES.--According to Solomon, a Nestorian bishop, the creation was planned from everlasting. (See chapters i-vii.)]

[The Creation. Second Day.]

And on the Second Day God made the Lower Heaven.
